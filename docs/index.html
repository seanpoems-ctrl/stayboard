How the numbers are worked out
Nights open for sale
Calendar nights since the unit's first stay, minus nights blocked for maintenance, personal use, or blocked with no reason recorded. Those blocked nights are left out of occupancy and are never counted as gaps.
Occupancy
Booked nights ÷ nights open for sale.
Average nightly rate
Room revenue ÷ booked nights, excluding the cleaning fee. Stays without a recorded price (some direct stays) are left out.
Revenue per available night
Room revenue ÷ nights open for sale. It moves with both price and occupancy.
Net revenue
What the platforms pay out after their commission or service fee, plus direct-stay income and cancellation fees kept. "After cleaning" also removes the cleaning fee, which is passed on to cleaners.
Gap nights
Unbooked nights with a stay on both sides (up to 14 nights). One- and two-night gaps are flagged, because they are the hardest to sell and the easiest to fix with a minimum-stay or price change.
Lead time
Days from booking to check-in, for Airbnb, Agoda and Booking.com bookings.
Direct stays
Taken from the host's calendar notes from 2023 onward. Earlier direct stays can't be identified and appear as blocked nights.
Estimated figures
Airbnb's fee split before Oct 2023 and Agoda's cleaning fee are worked out from the payout pattern. Direct-stay prices come from the host's own notes.
